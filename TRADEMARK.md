# Trademark and brand policy

The code of phplives.dev is free software, released under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). This page
is about the things the licence does not cover: the site's line, its art, and its
design and copy.

Nothing on this page takes away any right the MIT licence gives you over the code.

## What the MIT licence covers

The MIT licence covers the **source code** of phplives.dev: the build script, the
templates, the styles and the scripts. You may use it, copy it, change it, merge it
into your own work, publish it, and sell it, for free. You must keep the copyright
notice and the licence text with it.

## What this policy covers

The MIT licence is a copyright licence. It gives no right to use the site's name or
brand. Those are separate. This policy covers these things:

- The name **phplives.dev**, when it is used for a site or a project.
- The line **`PHP delivers. PHP lives!`**, as the line of this site.
- The art of the site. Each entry is drawn as a shipping label, with a "From" block,
  a postmark that reads "PHPLIVES.DEV" and "SHIPPED", wave lines, a barcode and a
  tracking number. Each label shows its stack as a short route code. The line at the top of the page sits on a label that looks stuck on. The
  summary looks like a packing slip, and the archive looks like a manifest sheet.
- The design and layout of the site, as a whole: the look above, with its colours,
  in the light and the dark theme.
- The written copy of the site, including the sentence that describes each entry.

These things identify this site. They are not part of the MIT grant.

## What this policy does not cover

- **The word "PHP".** "PHP" is a trademark of The PHP Group. This policy does not
  cover it. It covers the line `PHP delivers. PHP lives!` only as the line of this
  site. You can still say, in your own words, that PHP is alive. The words "PHP
  delivers" were [Damien Seguy's idea](https://x.com/faguo/status/2112245344285474818).
  Anyone can use them.
- **The projects on the list.** The name and the logo of each listed project belong
  to its owner. This site links to them. It does not own them. If you want to use
  them, ask their owners.
- **The fonts.** The site uses Archivo and JetBrains Mono. They belong to their
  makers and keep their own licences.
- **The facts in an entry.** A project's name, its makers, its date and its link are
  facts, and anyone may repeat them.

## What you can do without asking

You do not need permission for any of this, and you are welcome to it:

- Link to [phplives.dev](https://phplives.dev), or to any page or entry on it.
- Quote the line `PHP delivers. PHP lives!` when you talk about this site, or when
  you share a link to it.
- Share screenshots of the site, without changes, in a post, an article, a video or a
  talk, with a link back to the site.
- Write about the site, including in paid articles, courses or talks, and use its
  name to say what they are about.
- Say that your project is listed on phplives.dev, when it is.
- **Fork the repository on GitHub to contribute.** Nothing on this page applies to
  that. See "Contributing" below.
- Use the code for your own site, as the licence allows. See "Running your own site"
  below.

## What needs permission

Please ask first before you do any of this:

- Give **your own** site, project, domain, subdomain, app, social account or company
  a name that could be read as this site, or as an official version of it.
- Use the line `PHP delivers. PHP lives!` as the line or the title of your own site
  or product.
- Use the art of the site, such as the shipping labels or the postmark, as the logo,
  the icon or the main image of your own site or product. This includes art that you
  have changed.
- Say that your work is official, endorsed, approved or run by phplives.dev, when it
  is not.
- Register a trademark, a domain or an app-store name that is "phplives" or close
  enough to be confused with it.
- Sell merchandise with the name, the line or the art on it.

## Running your own site

You are free to build your own list of projects, for PHP or for any other language.
Nobody owns the idea, and a good list is welcome.

If you use this code to run your own site:

- Give it your own name and your own domain.
- Give it your own design and your own copy. Do not carry over the line or the art.
- Keep the copyright notice and the licence text, as the licence requires.
- You may say that phplives.dev inspired it. A link back is appreciated.

The reason is not ownership of the idea. A person who lands on your site may think it
is this one. Then they may send their entries here, report your bugs here, and judge
this site by pages it did not make.

## Contributing: nothing here applies

If you fork the repository to add an entry, fix a bug or open a pull request, **stop
reading. This page is not about you.**

Fork it, keep everything as it is, push your branch and open the pull request. Your
fork is meant to look like phplives.dev, because it is phplives.dev. This needs no
permission from anyone.

## Asking

Permission is usually easy to get. Often the answer is just "yes, and here is the
wording to use". Contact Alberto Arena through [albertoarena.it](https://albertoarena.it)
and say what you want to do.

Maybe you have already published something, and this page says it needs a change.
Please write anyway. The first message will always be a request, not a complaint.
